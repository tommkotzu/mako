Mako
your last file browser

Mako is a fork of GNOME Files and a left-hand, hotkey-first file manager for Linux.

Star Mako on GitHubDownload for Ubuntu

01

Main features

Tiles with their own tabs, a box that finds anything, and the panels only when you want them.

Tiles and tabs

Split the window. Every pane gets its own tabs.

KeysF3 right · Shift+F3 down · Alt+T next tile
MouseDrag a tab to any edge of a pane
Commander

Commander: find everything with Ctrl+E

Folders anywhere, files, commands and settings, in one box.

⌕
KeysCtrl+E · Shift+Enter opens in a split
MouseClick the path bar
Focus

Just your files, when you want.

Sidebar and inspector are optional. Hide them, and even their buttons, for a clean window.

KeysAlt+Q sidebar · Alt+E inspector
Four views

List, tree, Miller columns, grid.

04

Quality of life

The small things you notice every day.

Inspector

A rich inspector, with ratings and tags.

Preview, stars and tags you can set right there, and every detail of the selection.

KeysAlt+E
MouseView menu in the bottom bar, or its header button
Key hints

Every shortcut says its name.

Press a key and its name shows for a moment, so you learn them as you go.

Split RightF3
Tooltips

Buttons tell you their keys.

Hover any button and the tooltip names its shortcut.

Copies

Paste again; the next one waits.

Copies and moves show in a panel in the corner and run one after another.
